# Accessibility

This site targets [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) level AA ([RGAA](https://accessibilite.numerique.gouv.fr/) in France).

- **Status** — conformant, except some third-party content (e.g. Google Scholar badge on the research page) that I do not control.
- **Read aloud** — blog posts offer “Listen to this article” via your browser’s built-in text-to-speech. No audio is sent to this site; quality and pause support depend on your browser and OS.
- **France — recourse** — [Défenseur des droits](https://formulaire.defenseurdesdroits.fr/)

Something wrong? [Contact me](/contact/).
